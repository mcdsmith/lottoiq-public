About LottoIQ

Built on data. Grounded in honesty.

LottoIQ is a Canadian lottery analytics platform focused on transparency, integrity, and responsible play.

See How It Works

Our Story

LottoIQ was born out of frustration with lottery websites that make bold predictions and false promises. We believed there was a better way, one built on real historical data, transparent methodology, and complete honesty about what the numbers can and cannot tell us.

LottoIQ is the result: a lottery analytics platform that respects your intelligence and never misleads you.

Our Mission

NUMBERS, DRIVEN BY NUMBERS

Our mission is simple. Give Canadian lottery players access to the same historical data and statistical tools that were previously scattered across multiple sources; presented clearly, honestly, and without false promises. We help you make more intentional picks. We never claim to help you win.

Our Values

◈

Transparency

Every analysis we show includes the methodology behind it. No black boxes. No hidden algorithms.

◆

Honesty

Lottery draws are random. We say so clearly and prominently, not buried in fine print.

▽

Integrity

We will never claim our tools predict winning numbers. If a feature could mislead you, we won't build it.

The Data

LottoIQ covers four major Canadian lottery games: Lotto 6/49, Lotto MAX, Lottario and Ontario 49. Our databases contain decades of historical draw results sourced from lottery organizations. Data is updated weekly and manually verified before being added to the platform.

Responsible Play

We take responsible gambling seriously. LottoIQ is an entertainment and analytics platform not a gambling service. We encourage all players to set spending limits, play within their means, and seek help if gambling becomes a problem. Links to responsible gambling resources are available on our Responsible Gambling page.
