Frequently Asked Questions

What is the methodology behind LottoIQ's historical frequency analysis?

We count how often each number has appeared across thousands of historical draws. We then calculate patterns like sum ranges, spread between numbers, and odd/even balance. All analysis is based on historical data, we do not use machine learning or predictive modeling.

How accurate are the hot and cold number trackings?

Hot and cold numbers simply reflect how frequently each number has appeared in recent draws compared to historical averages. They are descriptive, not predictive. Lottery draws are truly random and past frequency does not influence future results.

Which Canadian lotteries are supported by LottoIQ?

LottoIQ provides comprehensive analytics on 4 popular Canadian draws, Lotto 6/49, Lotto MAX, Lottario, and Ontario 49.

Is LottoIQ a tool for guaranteed lottery wins?

No. LottoIQ is a lottery analytics and number generation platform for entertainment purposes. We provide historical data and statistical tools to help you make more informed number selections. We never claim to predict or guarantee winning numbers.

How is the data for Ontario 49 and Lottario updated?

We update our databases weekly using lottery database sources. Each update is verified before being added to the platform. Data is not updated in real-time.
