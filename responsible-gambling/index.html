Our Position

LottoIQ does not sell lottery tickets and is not a gambling service. We provide historical data analysis and number generation tools for entertainment purposes only. However we recognize that lottery play can become problematic for some people and we take that seriously.

Play Smart. Play Responsibly.

LottoIQ is an analytics platform for entertainment. We are committed to promoting responsible lottery play.

Know the Facts

Lottery draws are random

No system, algorithm, or historical analysis can predict lottery outcomes. Every draw is an independent random event.

Set a budget

Only spend what you can comfortably afford to lose. Treat lottery tickets as entertainment with a cost — not as an investment.

Play for fun

The enjoyment should come from the experience of playing — not from expecting to win. If winning feels necessary rather than exciting it may be time to reassess.

Know the odds

The odds of winning a major Canadian lottery jackpot are roughly 1 in 14 million. LottoIQ helps you make informed picks — it does not improve these odds.

Recognize the Signs

If any of the following apply to you or someone you know, please seek support:

Spending more on lottery tickets than you can afford
Chasing losses by buying more tickets
Hiding lottery spending from family or friends
Feeling anxious or irritable when not playing
Believing a system or tool guarantees wins
