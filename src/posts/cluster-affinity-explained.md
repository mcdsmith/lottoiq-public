*Tags: cluster affinity, due score, methodology*

Cluster Affinity shows how often a number has appeared in the same draws as the numbers that are currently performing the strongest. It is one of the four parts of the Due Score and can add up to 25 points.

## How It Works

LottoIQ recalculates Cluster Affinity whenever you change the game or the selected dataset:

1. **Find the Hot 10.** LottoIQ identifies the 10 numbers that have appeared most strongly in the selected period. The Hot 10 may be different in Last 30, Last 90, and All-time views.
2. **Review each draw.** LottoIQ checks how many Hot 10 numbers appeared in every draw. For example, a draw containing three Hot 10 numbers receives a count of 3.
3. **Give credit to the other numbers.** Every number in that draw receives credit for appearing alongside the Hot 10 numbers. More recent draws count more than older draws. A Hot 10 number does not receive credit for appearing alongside itself.
4. **Convert the result into points.** After all draws are reviewed, each number receives a Cluster Affinity score from 0 to 25. Numbers that have appeared more often with the Hot 10 receive a higher score.

## Simple Example

Suppose a draw contains six numbers, including three members of the current Hot 10. The other numbers in that draw receive credit for appearing with those three hot numbers. When a number repeatedly appears in draws containing several Hot 10 numbers, its Cluster Affinity score increases.

## How It Differs From Top Number Pairs

Top Number Pairs shows how often two specific numbers have appeared together. Cluster Affinity looks at something broader: how often one number has appeared alongside the entire current Hot 10 group. Because the Hot 10 changes with the selected dataset, the Cluster Affinity score can also change.
