*Not all numbers are created equally, at least lately.*

Every number in a lottery pool has the same mathematical odds on draw day. But in practice, some numbers have been showing up regularly in recent draws while others have gone quiet. The Hot & Cold Numbers section on LottoIQ puts both sides of that picture in front of you at once.

**How it works**

Two panels, side by side. Hot numbers are the top 6 most drawn in your selected dataset, the ones making regular appearances lately. Cold numbers are the bottom 6, barely seen, and conspicuously absent from recent results.

Both lists update automatically from real draw results, so what you're seeing reflects actual history, not a static snapshot.

**How players use it**

Some players chase the heat, reasoning that frequently drawn numbers are on a streak worth following. Others go the opposite direction and bet on a cold number "coming due." LottoIQ shows you both sides without pushing you toward either, the choice is yours.

**Dataset options**

Standard members see hot and cold numbers based on the last 30 draws. Insider members can switch to Last 90 or All-time, which can shift the lists significantly depending on the game. A number that looks cold over 30 draws might look perfectly average over its full history, or even hotter.
