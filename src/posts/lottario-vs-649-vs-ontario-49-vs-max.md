*Tags: comparison, odds, strategy*

Four Canadian lottery games, four different bets. "Best shot" can mean the best raw odds, the best value for your dollar, the biggest possible payout, or the most consistent prize structure, and Lottario, Lotto 6/49, Ontario 49, and Lotto MAX each win on a different one of those questions. Here's the honest, side-by-side comparison.

*Better odds do not mean good odds, and no game offers a reliable way to win. This comparison only explains differences in ticket structure, pricing, and published jackpot odds.*

*A pricing note: Lotto MAX's ticket price and format changed in April 2026, tickets are now $6 for four lines of 7 numbers from a 52-number pool, up from $5 for three lines. The figures below reflect the current 2026 structure for all four games.*

## The Numbers, Side by Side

| Game | Pool | Pick | Price | Draws/wk | Jackpot Odds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lottario | 1–45 | 6 (x2 sets) | $1 | 1 | ~1 in 4,072,530 |
| Lotto 6/49 | 1–49 | 6 | $3 | 2 | 1 in 13,983,816 |
| Ontario 49 | 1–49 | 6 | $1 | 2 | 1 in 13,983,816 |
| Lotto MAX | 1–52 | 7 (x4 lines) | $6 | 2 | ~1 in 33.4 million |

*A note on how to read the odds above: Lottario's approximately 1-in-4,072,530 figure reflects the two selections included with every $1 play, the odds for a single six-number selection are approximately 1 in 8,145,060. Lotto MAX's approximately 1-in-33.4-million figure similarly reflects all four lines included with the $6 ticket; one individual line has much longer odds on its own. These games are compared here by full ticket, not by individual line.*

## Best Odds of Winning the Jackpot

Lottario has the best odds of the four. A smaller 45-number pool, combined with getting two sets for every $1 play, gives it jackpot odds of about 1 in 4,072,530. Lotto 6/49 and Ontario 49 are tied at 1 in 13,983,816, since they share the same 49-number pool and 6-number pick. Lotto MAX has the steepest odds by a wide margin, about 1 in 33.4 million, the cost of a much bigger pool (52 numbers) and an extra pick (7 instead of 6).

## Best Value Per Dollar

Lottario again: $1 buys two sets, each with the best odds on this list. Ontario 49 is the next-best value, $1 for a single set with odds identical to 6/49's. Lotto MAX, at $6 for four sets of a much longer-odds game, costs the most per play of the four games, though each play now includes four lines instead of one.

## Biggest Jackpot Upside

Lotto MAX, and it isn't close. Jackpots start at $10 million and climb in $5 million increments to a $90 million cap. Once the jackpot passes $50 million, MaxMillions kick in, additional $1 million prizes, up to 90 of them at the $90 million cap. Nothing else on this list comes close to that upside.

## Most Consistent Jackpot Structure

Ontario 49 offers a fixed $2 million jackpot on every draw. Unlike a rolling jackpot, the advertised amount does not need to build over several draws. However, the jackpot is only paid when a ticket matches all six numbers. Lotto 6/49 comes closest to matching that structure with its Classic Jackpot, a $5 million prize advertised every draw, on top of a separate Gold Ball draw offering either a guaranteed $1 million prize or a second rolling jackpot that climbs from $10 million to a $68 million cap. Lottario's jackpot starts at $250,000 and only grows, rolling until someone wins it. Lotto MAX starts at $10 million and grows when the main jackpot is not won.

| Priority | Game structure |
| --- | --- |
