When Lotto MAX's advertised jackpot crosses $50 million, something genuinely changes about that draw, not just the size of the number on the sign. Here's exactly what turns on, what doesn't, and what it means for your odds.

*This reflects Lotto MAX's April 2026 format: $6 tickets, four lines of 7 numbers from a 52-number pool, jackpots from $10 million to a $90 million cap.*

## MaxMillions: The $50 Million Trigger

Once the main jackpot reaches $50 million, additional MaxMillions prizes get added to that draw, each worth $1 million on its own, drawn separately from the main jackpot numbers. If a $50 million-plus jackpot rolls over without being won, more MaxMillions prizes can be added to the following draws while the jackpot sits at or above that threshold. They're extra prizes stacked on top of the draw, not a change to the main jackpot's own odds.

## MaxPlus: The Prize That Scales Every Draw

New as of the April 14, 2026 format change, MaxPlus prizes appear in every single draw regardless of jackpot size, no $50 million threshold required. Each draw includes one $100,000 MaxPlus prize for every $1 million in that draw's advertised jackpot. A $10 million jackpot draw carries 10 MaxPlus prizes; at the $90 million cap, that's 90 MaxPlus prizes of $100,000 each.

## How the Two Stack Up

| Jackpot Size | MaxMillions Active? | MaxPlus Prizes | MaxPlus Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $10 million | No | 10 | $100,000 each |
| $30 million | No | 30 | $100,000 each |
| $50 million | Yes | 50 | $100,000 each |
| $90 million (cap) | Yes | 90 | $100,000 each |

## What This Means for Your Odds

None of it changes your odds of matching the main numbers for the jackpot itself, that's the same 1 in 33.4 million every single draw, MaxMillions and MaxPlus active or not. What changes is the number of additional prizes in play and the total amount being given away that night, more ways to win something, not better odds of winning the big one. MaxMillions and MaxPlus draws use the same random process as the main draw: independent, with no memory of past results.
