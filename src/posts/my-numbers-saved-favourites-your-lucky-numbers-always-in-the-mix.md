*An Insider feature that puts your personal picks on autopilot.*

Most people who play the lottery regularly have a set of numbers they always include, a birthday, an anniversary, a combination that just feels right. The problem is remembering to add them every single time, across every generated set. The My Numbers feature on LottoIQ solves that.

**How it works**

As an Insider member, you can save up to 2 of your own numbers directly in the generator. Once saved, those numbers are automatically locked into every set you generate, no re-entering them each session, no forgetting to include them, no manual overrides needed. The generator fills the remaining picks around your saved numbers using your chosen filters and historical frequency weighting. So your favourites are always in the mix, and the rest of the set is still built with the same analytical structure as any other generated pick.

**Flexibility when you want it**

Your saved numbers stay on your profile between sessions. If you want to run a set without them one time, just clear the field, they'll still be saved for next time. You can update or replace them whenever you like.

**How it fits with other filters**

My Numbers works alongside every other Insider filter. Combine it with a Top Pair lock and you could have up to 4 numbers pre-set before the generator fills the rest. Add an overdue number on top of that and you're building a genuinely personalised generation strategy rather than just clicking a button and hoping.

**Who it's for**

If you have numbers you've been playing for years and you're not ready to abandon them, My Numbers lets you keep them in every ticket while still applying the analytical filters that make the generator useful. It's the cleanest way to blend personal preference with data-informed structure.
