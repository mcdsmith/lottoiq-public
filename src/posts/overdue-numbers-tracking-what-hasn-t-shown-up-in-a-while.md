*The ones that haven't appeared in a while, and what you can do with that.*

Every lottery number has a draw count since it last appeared. The Overdue Numbers section on LottoIQ tracks exactly that, ranking every number in the pool by how long it's been absent from results. The longer the gap, the higher it ranks.

**How it works**

The overdue list is sorted automatically so the numbers with the longest absence sit at the top. It covers all four Canadian games on LottoIQ and updates after every draw, so you're always looking at current data.

**The feature that makes it useful**

You don't just look at the overdue list on LottoIQ, you can act on it directly. Tap up to 2 numbers from the list and they'll be locked into your next generated pick automatically. No copy-pasting, no second-guessing, no manually entering them into the generator. They're in, and the generator fills the rest of your set around them using your chosen filters.
