*What's free, what's $9.99 a month, and who actually benefits from upgrading.*

LottoIQ is free to use, full stop. Every game page, every core stat, works without an account. Insider is a $9.99/month (or $79.99/year) upgrade, and it's worth being upfront about what that money actually buys: not better odds. No lottery tool can honestly sell that, and the backtests on this blog prove it plainly. What Insider sells is depth, speed, and control for people who are already playing regularly. Here's exactly what that means, and who it's actually for.

## Who Actually Benefits from Insider

Four kinds of players get real, tangible value out of Insider. None of it involves an edge on the numbers.

- **The multi-line player.** If you play three, five, ten sets every draw, Standard's one-set-per-click generator means clicking Generate over and over. Insider generates 1 to 10 sets in a single click. That's a real time save, every draw, for as long as you play.
- **The data completionist.** Some players just want to see the whole picture, not a 30-draw window. Insider unlocks Last 90 and All-time across every stat, plus Top Number Pairs, which Standard members see locked behind an upgrade prompt rather than being able to open. If you like digging into the history for its own sake, this is where that lives.
- **The creature of habit.** If you've played the same birthday or anniversary numbers for years and don't want to abandon them, My Numbers & Saved Favourites locks up to 2 of your own numbers into every generated set automatically, no re-entering them each session, while the rest of the set still gets built with real filters.
- **The filter-first player.** Insider adds four generator filters Standard doesn't get: uniqueness (never-drawn-before combinations), spread control, locking in a historically frequent pair, and the My Numbers filter above. If you like fine-tuning exactly how a set gets built, these are the controls for that.

## Free for Every Member

None of this is a bait-and-switch. A full, genuinely useful version of LottoIQ is free, and always will be.

- Overdue Numbers and Did My Numbers Ever Win? always run against full draw history, every member, every game, no upgrade required.
- The Randomness Audit and the Randomness-in-Action Simulator on the Proof page are free for everyone. They exist to prove the stats don't predict anything, gating that behind a paywall would undercut the whole point.
- Frequency Heatmap, Hot & Cold Numbers, and Pattern Statistics are all available to Standard members, capped at the Last 30 draws. Due Score is available too, showing the top 5 numbers with a score, Insider adds the top 10 plus the full per-signal breakdown.
- The Number Generator's four core filters (sum range, odd/even balance, low/high balance, include overdue numbers) work for every member, generating one set at a time.

## Pricing

Insider runs $9.99/month or $79.99/year. The annual plan works out to about $6.67/month, roughly a third cheaper than paying monthly.
