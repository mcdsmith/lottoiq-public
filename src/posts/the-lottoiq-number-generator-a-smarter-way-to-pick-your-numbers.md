*Not a random number button, a filtered, frequency-weighted picker you control.*

Most lottery apps have a quick pick button. You tap it, you get numbers, you have no idea why those numbers were chosen. The LottoIQ Number Generator works differently, it lets you set your own criteria before a single number is selected, and then explains why each number made the cut.

**What every member gets**

Four filters are available to all Standard members:

1. **Sum range:** Choose Typical, Low, or High. Each range is calibrated against actual historical draw sums for that game, so you're not guessing at what "high" means, the typical range is shown right on the filter.
2. **Odd/Even balance:** Pick any combination: Any, 3/3, 4/2, 2/4, or heavier splits. The generator won't produce a set that doesn't match what you've chosen.
3. **Low/High balance:** Control how many numbers fall in the lower versus upper half of the pool.
4. **Include overdue numbers:** Lock in 1 or 2 of the most overdue numbers automatically, without cross-referencing the overdue list manually.

**What Insider members unlock**

Four additional filters become available with an Insider membership:

1. **Uniqueness:** Check "Never drawn before" to filter for combinations with no exact match in draw history.
2. **Spread:** Control whether your numbers cluster together or spread across the full pool. The most common historical spread is shown right on the filter so you know what typical looks like.
3. **Include a Top Pair:** Lock in a historically frequent number pair so both numbers are guaranteed in your generated set.
4. **My Numbers:** Save up to 2 of your own favourite numbers and have them automatically included in every generated set without re-entering them each time.
5. **Number Weighting:** Choose Hot to favour the most frequently drawn numbers, Cold to go against the grain with the most overdue numbers, or Neutral for no bias either way.

**What you get**

Hit Generate My Numbers and the result includes a plain-English explanation of why each number was included. Don't like the set? Hit Regenerate for a fresh pick using the same filters. Like it? Copy the numbers or save the pick in one tap.
