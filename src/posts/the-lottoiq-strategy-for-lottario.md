*Lottario is the underdog of Ontario lotteries. One dollar gets you two sets of numbers, one draw per week, and a jackpot that starts at $250,000 and rolls until someone wins it. It's not flashy but it has a tighter pool than any other game LottoIQ covers, and that changes the strategy meaningfully.*

**The Game in Numbers**

- **Pool:** 1–45
- **Pick:** 6 numbers
- **Draws:** Saturday only
- **One draw per week:** This is the critical difference.

**Why the Single Weekly Draw Changes Everything**

With only 52 draws a year (versus 100 for the other games), your dataset grows more slowly. That has two implications for how you use LottoIQ:

1. **The decay lambda is more aggressive on smaller datasets.** With fewer draws in the window, recent results carry more weight. A number that went quiet 10 draws ago is very cold by Lottario standards, that's 10 weeks of absence.
2. **Overdue numbers mean more here.** In a twice-weekly game, a 20-draw absence is 10 weeks. In Lottario, it's 20 weeks, nearly five months. The Due Score overdue signal hits differently on a smaller dataset.

**The LottoIQ Lottario Framework**

**Step 1: Lean heavily on the Overdue signal.** More than any other game, Lottario rewards attention to overdue numbers. Watch the Due Score overdue component specifically, if it's sitting at 20+ points on a number that hasn't appeared in 15+ draws, that's notable.

**Step 2: Tighter pool = stronger pairs data.** 45 numbers means pairs repeat more often than in a 49- or 52-ball game. Top Number Pairs is especially useful here. Cross-referencing your Due Score picks against the top pairs list should be your standard workflow.

**Step 3: Be conservative with ↓ Cooling numbers.** In a game with fewer draws, a cooling trend has had more time to develop and is less likely to reverse quickly. Avoid numbers that are ↓ Cooling unless their overdue score is exceptionally high (20+ points).

**Step 4: Use both sets.** Remember each $1 Lottario play gives you *two* sets. Use your Due Score top picks to anchor Set 1, then build Set 2 with the next tier of scores, particularly numbers that are Stable with strong overdue standing. Diversify across the two sets rather than doubling down on the same picks.
