*Lotto MAX is the big one. Jackpots that start at $10 million and can run all the way to $90 million. But with great jackpots comes a bigger pool, 52 numbers, pick 7, and odds of 1 in 33.4 million. The strategy here must account for that wider spread.*

**The Game in Numbers**

- **Pool:** 1–52
- **Pick:** 7 numbers
- **Draws:** Tuesday & Friday
- **Two draws a week:** similar data density to 6/49, but 3 more numbers to cover and 1 extra pick per set.

**Why MAX Needs a Different Approach**

Three extra numbers in the pool (49 vs 52) sounds minor. It's not. Those extra numbers at the top of the range, 50, 51, 52 appear less frequently in casual player selections, which means they're underrepresented in the historical data and worth watching specifically in the Due Score overdue signal.

Seven picks instead of six also means you can afford to be slightly more aggressive on cluster affinity, more room in your set means you can anchor a couple of high-affinity numbers alongside your Due Score leaders.

**The LottoIQ MAX Framework**

**Step 1: Due Score, but focus on the top 7, not top 10.** With 7 picks to fill, you don't need a deep bench. Zero in on the top 7 Due Score numbers where the score is driven by at least frequency plus one other signal, trend-only scores without frequency support are thinner on a 52-ball pool.

**Step 2: Pay attention to the 40–52 range specifically.** Numbers in this range are historically cooler in player selections and worth watching specifically in the Due Score overdue signal. If any number in the 45–52 band appears in your Due Score top 10 with an ↑ Rising or → Stable tag, it deserves a serious look.

**Step 3: Use cluster affinity as a tiebreaker.** When you have two numbers with similar total Due Score, take the one with the higher cluster-affinity component. On a 52-ball pool, co-occurrence patterns carry more signal — there's more room for clusters to form and persist.

**Step 4: Don't ignore MaxMillions implications.** When the jackpot climbs above $50 million, MaxMillions prizes kick in. More prizes in the draw means more winners across the number space. This doesn't change the math, but it's worth knowing that you're not only playing one jackpot.
