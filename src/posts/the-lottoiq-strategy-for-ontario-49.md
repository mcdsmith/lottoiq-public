*Ontario 49 is the local version of a classic. Same pool as Lotto 6/49 (1–49, pick 6), same draw days (Wednesday & Saturday), but at $1 a play instead of $3, and with a fixed $2 million jackpot every draw, guaranteed. It's not going to make you retire early, but the economics are friendlier, and the strategy is clean.*

**The Game in Numbers**

- **Pool:** 1–49
- **Pick:** 6 numbers
- **Draws:** Wednesday & Saturday
- **Fixed $2M jackpot:** no rollovers, guaranteed every draw

**How Ontario 49 Compares to Lotto 6/49**

The math is identical: 1 in 13,983,816 jackpot odds, same pool, same pick count. Because Ontario 49 draws on the same nights as 6/49, the historical dataset is just as rich. Same decay weighting, same signal quality, same framework applies.

The real difference is player volume. Fewer people play Ontario 49, which means jackpots are won less often and the pool of secondary prize money is split between fewer tickets. For the analytics, it doesn't matter, the numbers don't know which game they're in.

**The LottoIQ Ontario 49 Framework**

Ontario 49 is the closest game to a "default" LottoIQ strategy since it shares its pool and draw schedule with 6/49. Use the same framework, with one tweak:

**Step 1: Run Due Score and target the top 6 with mixed signals.** Same as 6/49: look for numbers scoring 70+ with frequency and at least one other signal firing. ↑ Rising + overdue is the sweet spot.

**Step 2: Pairs matter more at $1 a ticket.** Because Ontario 49 is cheaper to play, you can afford to buy a few more lines. If you're playing 3–4 sets, use Top Number Pairs to make sure you're not repeating the same two-number combination across every set. Spread the pairs across your lines.

**Step 3: Compare your picks against your 6/49 set.** If you're playing both games on the same night, there's no benefit to picking the exact same numbers. Use Ontario 49 as a chance to play your second-tier Due Score picks, the numbers ranked 7–12 that didn't make your 6/49 shortlist. More coverage, same draw night.

**Step 4: Treat the fixed jackpot as a feature, not a bug.** A $2M guaranteed prize every draw means the game never gets "stale" waiting for a big rollover. Every draw is equally worth playing. That consistency is actually useful for a data-driven approach, the incentive to play is constant, which means your dataset keeps growing at a steady pace.
