*Tags: trend, due score, strategy*

Every number has a Trend Tag beside its Due Score: Rising, Stable, or Cooling. The tag gives you a quick view of whether that number appeared more often, about the same, or less often in the recent part of your selected draw history.

## How Trend Tags Work

LottoIQ divides your selected draw history into two equal parts: the newer half and the older half. It then compares how often each number appeared in both halves, while giving slightly more importance to the most recent draws.

The comparison determines the tag and the Trend Points added to the number's Due Score:

| Tag | Trend Points | What It Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ↑ Rising | 25 / 25 | More than 20% higher in the newer half |
| → Stable | 12 / 25 | Within 20% of the older half |
| ↓ Cooling | 0 / 25 | More than 20% lower in the newer half |

## What Happens When a Number Did Not Appear?

If a number did not appear in the older half, LottoIQ cannot make a normal percentage comparison. In that case:

- If it also did not appear in the newer half, the number is marked Stable.
- If it appeared in the newer half, it is marked Rising.

## Why the Tag Can Change

Trend Tags are based only on the draw range you are viewing. For example, Last 30 compares the newest 15 draws with the previous 15. If you switch to Last 90 or All-Time, the comparison changes, so the same number may receive a different tag.

## What Each Tag Means

**Rising:** The number appeared noticeably more often in the newer half of the selected draw range. It receives 25 Trend Points.

**Stable:** The number appeared at about the same rate in both halves. It receives 12 Trend Points.

**Cooling:** The number appeared noticeably less often in the newer half. It receives 0 Trend Points.
